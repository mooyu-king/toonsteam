Privacy Policy

This policy describes the features implemented on this guide. Last updated November 5, 2026.

Search and accounts

Title searches and format filters run in your browser against our small local collection. We do not send your search text as a custom analytics event. There are no user accounts, payment forms or public comments on this site.

Traffic analytics

We use an analytics script served from data.1back.link, with events sent to its /api/send endpoint, to understand visits and use of the site. Ordinary network and browser information may be processed by the service. We do not claim that visiting a website involves no technical data processing.

The install helper may report events such as opening installation help, installation availability and copying the site link. These events do not include your search query, email address or credentials.

Installation and browser storage

Your browser may save the installed web app, icons and ordinary HTTP cache. Installation does not download external anime episodes. On the production domain, a service worker caches interface assets and up to eight recently visited pages for up to seven days. Offline copies can be older than the current online version; review dates remain visible. An offline fallback explains when a page has not been saved. Third-party pages, streams and analytics are not cached by this worker. You can remove an installed app and clear browser site data through your browser settings.

External platforms

Following a source link leaves this website. The destination has its own privacy practices, account requirements and regional rules. Our analytics integration and external title links are not secret or private connections.

Email

If you email us, your email provider and the recipient process the message and any information you include. Send only what is needed to explain your question. Contact mooyuking@gmail.com about privacy questions or deletion requests relating to correspondence.
