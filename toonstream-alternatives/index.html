Other ways to find your next anime

Start with the kind of information you need, then confirm the exact title on the destination platform.

We keep this list short. These are different starting points, not a ranking or a promise that every Toonstream title is available elsewhere.

ANIME-FOCUSED CATALOG

Crunchyroll

Useful when you want a series page with seasons and listed audio or subtitle options. Our selection links to Naruto, SPY x FAMILY, Demon Slayer and JUJUTSU KAISEN listings.

Before you continue: check your country, the specific season, audio and current access plan. A language listed for a show does not establish availability for every episode.

FILM & SERIES PLATFORM

Netflix

A starting point for selected anime films and other series. We link to A Whisker Away and a UK listing for A Silent Voice.

Before you continue: catalogs and language options depend on location. Confirm the title in your own account and region before choosing a subscription.

What about Anime World, Toonstar and other names?

A name appearing in related searches is not a quality or safety endorsement. We do not add services to this list without enough evidence to describe their purpose accurately.

Compare the right things

  • Exact title and version, not just a familiar poster.
  • Audio versus subtitles, and whether your season is covered.
  • Availability in your country and the current price or plan.
  • The actual destination of the next link.
